# Metrik Database Nusantara
**Descriptive & Prescriptive Business Data Analytics Consulting**

Kami membantu pelaku usaha—khususnya sektor **Food & Beverage (F&B)**—mengubah catatan transaksi mentah menjadi keputusan strategis untuk meningkatkan margin laba, mengoptimalkan harga menu, dan menekan pemborosan rantai pasok.

---

### Layanan & Metodologi Analitik
* **Margin & Costing Audit:** *Prime Cost Analysis*, *Activity-Based Costing (ABC)*, & *Menu Engineering*.
* **Pricing & Promo Optimization:** *Price Elasticity of Demand (Log-Log Regression)* & *Market Basket Bundling*.
* **Supply Chain & Inventory Control:** *Dynamic EOQ-ROP*, Audit *Inventory Shrinkage*, & *Newsvendor Production*.
* **Customer & Labor Analytics:** *BG/NBD & Gamma-Gamma CLV* serta Optimasi *Shift* Kru.

---

### Tech Stack
`PostgreSQL` • `Python (pandas, statsmodels)` • `Microsoft Excel (Interactive Simulators)` • `Tableau` • `SQL`
